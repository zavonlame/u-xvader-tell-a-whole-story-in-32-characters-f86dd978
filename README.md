Entry for [Tell a Whole Story in 32 Characters](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-tell-a-whole-story-in-32-characters-f86dd978) — one Wi-Fi network name of at most 32 characters that lands a joke and implies a whole story.

## The entry

`MyExChangedThePasswordAgain` — 27 characters.

A petty post-breakup saga in one line: she changed the password. Again. The "again" is the story — this is not the first time, and it will not be the last.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) holds the name as a literal, checks it against the 1–32 character gate, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
